# Pincode Tycoon: Mumbai Edition

Master the Mumbai property market in this real-estate simulation game designed to teach children financial literacy through "Stealth Learning."

## 🏠 The Philosophy: Stealth Learning

Traditional education can often feel monotonous. **Pincode Tycoon** is built on the principle that children learn best when they don't realize they are being taught. By choosing Real Estate—a topic children see in their daily lives (the homes they live in, the buildings they pass)—we create a bridge between the game and the real world.

The goal is to maintain engagement by ensuring the simulation feels like a game first and a classroom never.

---

## 🎓 Core Curriculum

The simulation integrates complex real-world concepts into simple, turn-based mechanics:

### 🏢 1. Residential vs. Commercial
- **Residential (Flats/Villas):** Lower rental yields but high demand and stability. Consider it the "safe" bet for steady growth.
- **Commercial (Shops/Offices):** High rental income but carries the risk of vacancy. If a business leaves, your cash flow stops!

### 🏗️ 2. Project Lifecycle
- **Under-Construction:** Cheaper entry price with high appreciation potential, but carries the "Risk of Delay."
- **Ready-to-Move:** More expensive, but starts generating rental income immediately.

### 📈 3. Appreciation & Infrastructure
Value doesn't grow in a vacuum. In India, infrastructure is the primary driver. Properties near new **Metro Stations, Airports, or Highways** see accelerated growth.

### ⚠️ 4. The "Sticker Price" Trap
Children learn that the listed price isn't the final cost:
- **Stamp Duty & Registration:** An extra 5%–7% fee to the government.
- **Maintenance (HOA):** Quarterly fees paid for security and upkeep, even if the property is vacant.
- **Property Tax:** Annual dues to the local Municipal Corporation (BMC/BBMP).

### 🛡️ 5. Legal Safeguards: RERA
The **Real Estate Regulatory Authority (RERA)** is the ultimate protector. Buying non-RERA properties might be cheap, but carries a 30% risk of the developer disappearing.

### 📐 6. Area Terminology (Loading Factor)
What you see isn't always what you get. The game teaches the difference between:
- **Carpet Area:** The actual floor space you can walk on.
- **Built-up Area:** Carpet + Walls.
- **Super Built-up:** The lobby, lifts, and gym. If a builder offers 2000 sq ft but has a 40% loading factor, you only get 1200 sq ft of home!

### 🏛️ 7. Builder Reputation
Different properties have different trust scores based on whether they are built by **Private developers** or the **Government (MHADA)**, and their historical delivery track record.

---

## 💰 Key Mechanics

- **Passive Income:** Rent that works for you while you sleep.
- **Cash Flow:** Managing the balance between rent and maintenance to avoid "Negative Cash Flow."
- **Bankruptcy:** If liquid cash drops below ₹0, assets are forcibly liquidated at a 10% discount.
- **Goal:** Reach a net worth of ₹1 Crore (or maximize it) within 20 quarters.

## 🚀 Future Roadmap

- **Difficulty Levels:** A slider to introduce realistic market crashes, interest rate hikes, and more complex assessments.
- **Debt & EMI:** Introducing home loans and interest rates to teach children about leverage and interest costs.
- **Realistic Crashes:** Dynamic market events that can wipe out equity, teaching risk diversification.
